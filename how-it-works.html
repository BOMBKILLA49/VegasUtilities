How Vegas Utilities works

We're a matching service, not a contractor. Here's exactly what happens from the moment you submit your project to the moment you're comparing quotes.

1

Submit your project details

Fill out our short project form: your contact info, the property location, the type of work you need (earthwork, sewer, water, storm drain, or a combination), and your rough timeline. Takes about three minutes.

2

We review and clarify scope

We look over your submission and, if needed, follow up with a quick call or email to understand the site and confirm which type of contractor is the right fit.

3

We introduce you to vetted contractors

We connect your project with one or more licensed, insured contractors from our partner network who specialize in the scope you need and work in your area of the Valley.

4

You compare quotes and choose

The contractor(s) we introduce you to will contact you directly to walk the site, provide a quote, and — if you choose to move forward — contract with you directly. You're never obligated to hire anyone.

What we do

  • Provide free educational resources about site development
  • Review project submissions for basic scope and fit
  • Introduce qualified projects to vetted, licensed contractors
  • Maintain a small, pre-screened partner network — not an open directory

What we don't do

  • Perform earthwork, grading, or utility installation ourselves
  • Act as a general contractor or subcontractor on any project
  • Sign construction contracts or collect payment for construction work
  • Guarantee pricing, availability, or the outcome of any project

How we choose the contractors we recommend

We work with a limited, hand-selected group of licensed civil and site-work contractors in the Las Vegas Valley. See our About & Trust page for the criteria we use before we'll refer any contractor to a property owner.
