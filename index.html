What we do

A local resource for site development — not a contractor

Site development work — grading, earthwork, sewer, water, and storm drain installation — is one of the most misunderstood parts of any construction project in the Las Vegas Valley. Scope gets missed, soil conditions surprise people, and permitting between the City of Las Vegas, Henderson, North Las Vegas, and unincorporated Clark County can trip up even experienced developers.

Vegas Utilities exists to help you get it right from the start. We publish plain-language guides on what these projects involve locally, then match qualified projects with a short list of contractors we've already vetted — licensed, insured, and experienced in Southern Nevada's soil and regulatory environment.

We don't perform construction work ourselves. Every contract, quote, and job is handled directly between you and the licensed contractor we introduce you to.

How we vet our partners

Built for property owners & developers who need:

  • Rough grading or mass excavation for a new site
  • Sewer lateral or main line installation and connection
  • Water service line installation or upsizing
  • Storm drain, retention, or drainage system work
  • Full site utility packages for commercial or residential development
  • A second opinion before signing with a contractor
Project types

What falls under site development

These are the core scopes we help Las Vegas Valley property owners plan for and get matched on.

Earthwork & Grading

Mass excavation, rough and fine grading, cut-and-fill, and pad preparation for buildings, roads, and parking areas.

Sewer Line Installation

Sanitary sewer mains, laterals, manholes, and connections coordinated with the local sewer authority.

Water Line Installation

Domestic and fire service water lines, meters, and connections to the local water district's system.

Storm Drain & Drainage

Storm drain lines, detention/retention basins, and drainage systems built to Clark County flood control standards.

Full Site Preparation

Combined earthwork and utility packages that get a raw parcel ready for vertical construction.

Permitting Coordination

Guidance on which agency handles what, and what to expect during plan review and inspections.
