What's included in earthwork & grading

Earthwork and grading is the foundation-setting phase of nearly every construction project — the work that turns a raw parcel into a stable, engineered building pad. Contractors we match you with typically handle:

  • Site clearing and demolition of existing structures, vegetation, or debris
  • Mass excavation and cut-and-fill grading to design elevations
  • Soil compaction to the density specified in your geotechnical report
  • Rough grading for building pads, roads, and parking areas
  • Fine grading and final surface preparation before paving or foundation work
  • Erosion and dust control during construction, as required by local permits

Why earthwork in the Las Vegas Valley requires local experience

Soil conditions across the Valley vary more than most people expect. Caliche — a hard, cemented layer of calcium carbonate common in desert soils — shows up at different depths from one parcel to the next, and can require ripping or specialized equipment to excavate. Some areas also have variable rock or pockets of expansive clay. A contractor who regularly works in Las Vegas, Henderson, North Las Vegas, and unincorporated Clark County has already seen how these conditions affect schedule and cost — which matters more here than it would in a market with more uniform soil.

When you need an earthwork & grading contractor

  • You're developing a raw or vacant parcel and need it prepped for construction
  • Your civil engineering plans call for cut/fill work to reach design elevations
  • You need a pad built to specific compaction standards before foundation work
  • An existing site needs regrading for drainage or expansion
  • You're preparing a site for underground utility installation (sewer, water, storm drain)

If your project also needs underground utilities, many owners prefer a single contractor to handle grading and utilities together since the sequencing is tightly linked — see our full site development page.

Typical timeline

Mass excavation and rough grading on a mid-size Las Vegas Valley commercial site commonly runs one to four-plus weeks, depending on site size, soil conditions, and cut/fill balance. Frequently asked questions

What does an earthwork and grading contractor actually do?

An earthwork and grading contractor moves and compacts soil to turn raw land into a stable, engineered pad — cutting high areas, filling low areas, and compacting to the density your geotechnical report specifies so the site is ready for foundations, pavement, and underground utilities.

Why is earthwork different in the Las Vegas Valley compared to other markets?

Southern Nevada soils often include caliche along with variable rock and, in some areas, expansive clay. These conditions can require different equipment and methods than looser soils found elsewhere, which is part of why local experience matters when choosing a contractor.

Do I need a geotechnical report before getting a grading quote?

It's strongly recommended. A geotechnical (soils) report tells the contractor what they're actually working with, which leads to a more accurate quote and fewer change orders once excavation starts.

How long does grading typically take on a commercial site?

It varies widely with site size and soil conditions, but mass excavation and rough grading on a mid-size commercial site in the Las Vegas Valley commonly takes one to four or more weeks. See our costs and timelines guide for more detail.
